# Thesis compliance checklist - 2026/27/1

This checklist records the University of Debrecen Faculty of Informatics
requirements that apply to the final thesis and its submission. It should be
reviewed whenever the thesis document is edited and again before the supervisor
receives the submission copy.

## Fixed submission dates

- Standard deadline: **4 November 2026, 23:59:59**.
- Late submission ends: **18 November 2026, 23:59:59**.
- Late fee: HUF 800 per day in the first week and HUF 1,600 per day in the
  second week.
- After 18 November 2026, submission for this semester's final examination is
  not possible.

## Document structure

- [ ] Title page.
- [x] Word table-of-contents field included, using decimal numbering and showing
      chapters, sections, subsections, bibliography and page numbers. In the
      current Faculty-formatted draft, acknowledgements and the abstract precede
      the table of contents.
- [ ] Abstract/review of exactly 5-6 sentences for the DEA record; also keep a
      matching polished thesis abstract if the final document includes one.
- [ ] Introduction of approximately 2-3 pages covering topic choice,
      theoretical and practical importance, objectives and methods.
- [x] Main assessed part is 37 pages in the supervisor-revised draft (Chapter 1
      through the end of the bibliography).
- [ ] Conclusion of approximately 2-3 pages, including objectives that were not
      demonstrated and their justified explanation.
- [ ] Bibliography containing every source actually used.
- [ ] Appendices limited to approximately 8-10 pages.
- [x] Optional acknowledgements included.
- [x] Mandatory AI-use declaration at the end of the thesis, using the Faculty's
      official declaration form/wording.

## Formatting

- [ ] Times New Roman.
- [ ] Main text 12 pt.
- [ ] Headings/title-page elements may use 14 pt and 16 pt.
- [ ] Line spacing 1.5.
- [ ] Paragraphs justified.
- [ ] Left, upper and lower margins: 3 cm.
- [ ] Right margin: 2 cm.
- [x] Final draft visually checked for page breaks, tables, figures, captions,
      cross-references, page numbering and table-of-contents accuracy.

## Academic integrity and AI-use record

- [ ] AI is used only as an aid and does not replace the student's meaningful
      academic contribution.
- [ ] Every factual statement, citation, result and interpretation is verified
      by the student against primary data or an appropriate source.
- [ ] Maintain a working AI-use log recording tool name, model/version when
      available, date, information or prompt supplied, purpose, output used and
      the student's verification or revision.
- [ ] The final declaration accurately describes use for planning, code review,
      debugging, analysis support, language editing and drafting assistance as
      applicable.
- [ ] AI-generated references are never accepted without independently checking
      the cited source.
- [ ] Personal, confidential or sensitive information is not entered into an AI
      service without lawful authority.
- [ ] The official Faculty declaration is included at the end of the thesis.
- [ ] The declaration is not hidden in acknowledgements and is not replaced by
      a vague statement such as "AI was used for editing".

## Submission sequence - do not reorder

1. [ ] Confirm exact title equality between Neptun, thesis and DEA metadata.
2. [ ] Email the completed thesis to the supervisor; include the student's name
       and exact thesis title in the email.
3. [ ] Supervisor emails acceptance to the student and `to@inf.unideb.hu`.
4. [ ] Upload the supervisor-approved PDF to DEA.
5. [ ] Submit the Neptun request `IK Thesis submission / Statement_2026/27/1`;
       enter the thesis title, not the student's name.
6. [ ] Wait for the Thesis Statement/DEA upload to show accepted status.
7. [ ] Upload the same thesis through the Faculty's Microsoft form.
8. [ ] Confirm that the submission date appears in Neptun; no separate success
       email is promised.
